I decided I would do it. I also decided I wouldn't care if it was really good. Publishing something every day was the goal. Publishing something great every day? Not the goal.

By the way, I have the Substack feature turned on where instead of having to pay to subscribe you can just share it a lot and earn yourself a paid subscription anyway! Hot! Strong design choice, Mills Baker .

Here is the secret sauce:

I write every single day.

I commit to publishing something every single day.

I stop holding myself to the standard of writing something great every day.

Repeat, and Follow The Metrics:

I have 105 pieces in various phases of partial completion. The thing you see written on any given day is not necessarily the thing I wrote on that day. Some of the things you read started many months ago.

I also have some academic papers I am writing.

I also have at least one book I'm working on.

When you write every day, you get used to writing every day, which means the average quality goes up, for me.*

Writing improves with practice, thus more writing equals plausibly better writing?

Just get the thing out.

*typos will occur.

That's it. That's the post.
