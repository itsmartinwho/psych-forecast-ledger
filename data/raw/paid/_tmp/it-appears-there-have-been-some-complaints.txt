Editors Note: the following is part of the significant document dump after the fall of Sauron revealing the myriad complaints and issues raised even during Sauron’s tenure.

Mordor Human Resources Department One Dark Tower Mordor, Middle-earth

Subject: Grievance Against Staff of Mordor and Sauron for Abuse and Harassment

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to formally file a grievance against the staff of Mordor and, in particular, Sauron, for their continual abuse and harassment of my person. I am a longtime resident of Middle-earth and have been working under Sauron's rule for many years now. Throughout my tenure, I have been subjected to innumerable instances of harassment and mistreatment, which I can no longer tolerate. I have decided to raise my concerns with the Mordor Humanoid Resources Department in the hope that the situation will be properly addressed and remedied.

My grievances primarily stem from the excessive workload and impossible expectations placed upon me by Sauron, who has been forcing me to incessantly search for the One Ring. The constant pressure and mental anguish associated with this task have caused me to develop a severe case of split personality, with my alter ego, Gollum, now dominating my existence. The environment in Mordor has deteriorated my mental health to the extent that I feel I am no longer in control of my own thoughts and actions.

In addition, the working conditions in Mordor are hazardous and life-threatening. The barren landscape, polluted air, and lack of sunlight have taken a toll on my physical health, exacerbating my already weakened state. The Orcs, who are also under Sauron's employ, have made my life a living nightmare by constantly berating, threatening, and attacking me. Their behavior towards me is abhorrent, and it is unacceptable that such behavior goes unchecked by management.

Despite my attempts to raise these concerns with Sauron directly, I have been met with disdain and outright dismissal. He has repeatedly told me that my suffering is inconsequential and that I am expendable, which has only served to exacerbate my anxiety and depression. This lack of empathy and understanding from my superior is both demoralizing and dehumanizing.

I kindly request that the Mordor Humanoid Resources Department investigate this matter and take appropriate action against those responsible for the harassment and abuse I have endured. I believe that everyone has the right to be treated with dignity and respect, regardless of their position or the tasks they have been assigned.

Thank you for your attention to this serious matter. I am hopeful that your intervention will result in a safer and more supportive work environment for myself and others in Mordor. Please feel free to contact me at the phone number or email address provided above if you require any additional information or clarification.

Sincerely,

Sméagol/Gollum
