Meeting your readers is exciting
OWEN SCOTT MUIR, M.D.
MAY 28, 2023
∙ PAID
1

Writers—keep at it. I met some of my readers tonight. The microfame was…frankly, shocking. I took some pictures! When they heard the author of THE Frontier Psychiatrists newsletter was in attendance at a family gathering, the most remarkable thing occurred:

The hosts of a family gathering couldn’t believe their luck! Their friends raised them into the air!

There was dancing, shouting, joy!

The revelry was uninhibited.

It was clear: people are thrilled to be in the presence of a leading microblogging compliance nerd.

It’s nice to bring so much joy into people’s lives.

—Owen Scott Muir, M.D.
