Imposter syndrome is pop psychology term for worrying that people are going to find out you're not the real thing.

There is no international classification of disease code for imposter syndrome (with the possible exception of an extremely rare Capgras syndrome?)

Most individuals who describe themselves as worrying about imposter syndrome probably have a different problem, from a “taxonomy of validated psychiatric diagnosis” standpoint.

That having been said, I would argue there's a cure. And it's simple.

Actually be an imposter.

Impostors know they are faking. They do not live in doubt. 0% of actual imposters have imposter syndrome. Imposter syndrome, ironically, is only a disorder of non-imposters.

Yes.

I didn't say it was a good cure.
