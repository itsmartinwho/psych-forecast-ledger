To: Senior Management, Compliance Department, Humanoid Resources, Mordor Corporation Date: May 2, The Third Age

Dear Colleagues,

We have recently received an incident report detailing the events surrounding the fall of Gandalf, one of the Maia, during his confrontation with Durin's Bane. The purpose of this After Action Report is to outline the severe concerns raised by Humanoid Resources regarding potential OSHA violations during this incident.

Summary of the Incident:

Gandalf and his companions were navigating the mines of Moria when they encountered Durin's Bane, a Balrog, on the Bridge of Khazad-dûm.

Gandalf engaged in a what even casual observers would note was a fierce battle with Durin's Bane, which culminated in both of them falling into a deep chasm.

Gandalf eventually defeated Mr. Durin's Bane. In doing so, he suffered significant injuries and exhaustion in the process, resulting in a status change, leading to his new “Gandalf the White” status.

There is, for good or ill, a video record from the encounter:

Concerns and Potential OSHA Violations:

Lack of Safety Equipment and Precautions: It has been reported that neither Gandalf nor Durin's Bane was provided with or utilized any safety equipment during their confrontation, such as harnesses or fall protection systems. This apparent disregard for safety protocols raises serious concerns about the working conditions for the Maia involved.

Unsafe Work Environment: The location of the confrontation, the Bridge of Khazad-dûm, lacked appropriate safety measures such as guardrails, proper lighting, and clear markings. This hazardous environment contributed to the likelihood of falls and injuries during the battle.

Inadequate Supervision: There was no designated safety officer or supervisor present during the incident, which may have contributed to the disregard for safety protocols and the absence of appropriate safety measures. Reportedly, Perrigrin “Perrin” Took was the safety officer on official paperwork, which is concerning in and of itself, given his last performance review grimly concluded with the comment:

“fool of a Took!”

followed by no performance improvement plan, despite this serious admonition.

Insufficient Training: It is unclear whether Gandalf and other Maia have received proper safety training and education concerning the risks and hazards associated with their work. No record can be found of Gandalf completing his employee handbook and there is a glaring lack on a employment agreement. The safety protocol, even for crossing “inherently dangerous” structures like the aforementioned bridge are clearly documented in the employee handbooks second age update, V1.2.

Recommended Actions

:

Conduct a thorough investigation into the incident and identify all OSHA violations and areas of non-compliance.

Provide comprehensive safety training to all Maia and other personnel involved in similar activities or working in hazardous environments.

Implement and enforce safety protocols and equipment requirements in all areas of operation, including the use of fall protection systems, proper lighting, and guardrails.

Designate safety officers or supervisors to oversee high-risk activities and ensure compliance with OSHA standards and company policies.

We urge Senior Management and the Compliance Department to take these concerns seriously and address the identified OSHA violations to ensure the safety and well-being of all Maia and other personnel involved in our organization's activities. Your attention to this matter is crucial for maintaining a safe and compliant working environment.

Sincerely,

Shelob, SVP, Humanoid Resources Specialist

Mordor Inc.
