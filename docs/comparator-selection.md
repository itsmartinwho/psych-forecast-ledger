# Comparator selection (research scan, 2026-09-13)

Recommendation from the scan: Josh Hardman (Psychedelic Alpha)
Runner-up: Matthew W. Johnson (Sheppard Pratt, formerly Johns Hopkins)

Pick Josh Hardman. He has the most dated, checkable, in-scope predictions of any candidate not already covered: an estimated 40 or more across annual outlook pieces (Feb 2022, Feb 2023, Jan 2025, Jan 2026), quarterly briefings, interviews, and X posts. His claims cover all five core areas: regulatory (approvals in 2026, plural), clinical readouts (MAPS Phase 3 timing), company and market (a dozen company failures in H1 2023; limited M&A), policy (Oregon, Colorado, New Mexico), and payer questions. He publishes self-grades of his prior-year calls, which makes scoring transparent. He fills the analyst archetype, which neither Owen Scott Muir (clinician-skeptic) nor Christian Angermayer (investor-promoter) nor Rick Doblin (advocate, researched separately) supplies. His only weakness is prominence: high in the field, low with the general public. Runner-up is Matthew W. Johnson. He gives the academic archetype, has prominence 4, and a decade-long trail of dated approval calls (2015 'at least ten years'; 2021 'MDMA within 3 years'; 2024 'eventually, even if not by August'), several already resolved. He loses to Hardman on volume and on company and payer coverage. Rejected: Nolan Williams (died October 2025); Ben Sessa (12-month licence suspension); Sabbagh and Huberman (almost no dated forecasts); Carhart-Harris and Ferriss (aspirational language, few dates); Brand (same firm as Angermayer, no contrast); Emerson (out of role, one-program record); Nath (corporate guidance, insider overlap with Angermayer); Pollan (prominence 5 but only a handful of dated calls; consider him only if public name recognition outweighs scorable volume). Doblin remains the strongest figure overall and should stay in the separate workstream.

Decision (user, 2026-09-13): Rick Doblin joins Christian Angermayer. Josh Hardman stays optional for a later phase.
